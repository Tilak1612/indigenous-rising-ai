## Proposed copy for the guide

Replaces nothing. It is added after the illustrative table, and the
illustrative made-up numbers stay labelled as such.

> **What some lenders actually publish (read 2 November 2026)**
>
> - **First Peoples Economic Growth Fund (Manitoba):** for its Business
>   Contribution Fund, at least 10% of the project in cash equity from you, a
>   commercial loan of at least 40%, and a non-repayable contribution of up to
>   40% of eligible costs, to $99,999 for an individual.
> - **Waubetek (northeastern Ontario):** a 10% unencumbered equity
>   contribution from the applicant.
> - **Alberta Indian Investment Corporation:** your own equity as a portion of
>   the total financing, with no percentage published.
> - **Tale'awtxw Aboriginal Capital Corporation (Coast Salish territory):**
>   its funding supplements your equity and financing and cannot be applied
>   for on its own.
> - **Clarence Campeau Development Fund (Saskatchewan Métis):** its equity
>   grant needs an approved loan and is not available on its own.
>
> These are each lender's own rules on the date shown. Other lenders set
> different ones, so confirm with the institution that serves you.
